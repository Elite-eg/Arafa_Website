## 1. COMPANY IDENTITY / BRANDING OVERVIEW
===================================================================

- Current / New Brand Name: **Al Jazira for Imdad Smart Company**
- Arabic Name: شركة ألجزيرة للأمداد الذكية
- Former Name: **IMDAD Group** (also referred to as "IMDAD for Smart Buildings & Construction of Public Buildings")
- Parent Company: **Arafa Engineering** (ARFA) — Al Jazira for Imdad Smart Company is positioned as a subsidiary brand of Arafa Engineering.
- Industry: MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) Construction, General Contracting, Firefighting Systems, Smart Systems Integration.
- Region of operation: Gulf region (primarily Kingdom of Saudi Arabia — KSA), with past projects in Dubai (UAE) and Qatar.
- Years of experience: 45+ years (under the IMDAD name).
- Tagline / Motto: "Developing and serving society with the highest levels of professional integrity and commitment."

### Logo Redesign Notes
- The logo was redesigned to create a stronger visual connection with the parent company, Arafa Engineering, while maintaining a distinct identity for Al Jazira for Imdad Smart Company as a subsidiary brand.
- The previous logo relied heavily on thin lines and a lightweight visual style, which did not fully reflect the strength and nature of the company's work in construction supplies.
- The new logo was developed with a bolder, more solid visual direction, inspired by the identity and spirit of the parent company's (ARFA) logo — creating a clear relationship between both brands while giving Al Jazira its own recognizable identity.
- Before: Thin-line icon logo (house/building + tree/plant icon) with Arabic text "شركة ألجزيرة للأمداد الذكية" and English "Aljazira for Imdad Smart Company."
- After: Bolder, more solid geometric logo combining building/skyline shapes with the wordmark "ALJAZIRA" in bold black lettering, subtitle "FOR IMDAD SMART COMPANY," and Arabic text beneath.
- Brand icon motif used across the current website: a minimalist line-art icon combining a house/building outline, a tree, and rolling landscape/field lines (used as a favicon/logo element throughout the site).

## 4. LEGACY "WELCOME" / COMPANY HISTORY (from old IMDAD profile)
===================================================================
Visionary founder Mr. Rashwan Mahmoud established IMDAD GROUP. His vision was to create a large and leading Electromechanical company; he believed that hard work could make this a reality, and aimed to leave a legacy for future generations of professionals.

Today, IMDAD is a story of success built on shared values and ambition. World-renowned and recognized for its excellence in the industry, IMDAD has substantially grown over the years. Starting as IMDAD sanitary works in KSA, IMDAD diversified, constantly following the market. For over 45 years, the IMDAD Team has been reinforcing its international presence in the Gulf, expanding its in-house expertise and enlarging its scope of MEP Engineering services.

IMDAD GROUP provides a complete range of Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing services through a highly experienced Engineering & Project Management team, fully complying with time, cost, and quality requirements of projects. ### Company Description (extended)
IMDAD GROUP is the flagship of the MEP Construction Sector, a leading business house with a wide business interest in the Gulf Region. IMDAD Group is popularly known in the Mechanical and Electrical construction sector, providing complete, collaborative, end-to-end solutions, infusing life into new buildings and sustaining life in existing ones.

Nearly 45 years of IMDAD Team operations in Gulf markets — IMDAD has rapidly expanded and grown from strength to strength, delivering exceptional performance and earning a reputation as a highly dependable specialist MEP contractor in the Gulf region.

The company also offers clients high-performance facility operation that improves productivity, reduces energy consumption, and reduces operating costs, thereby providing long-term value.

IMDAD has developed to satisfy the construction market by training and qualifying its engineers to a very high standard in the MEP area. This enables it to offer three-quarters of the complete MEP package to its clients through its own staff, which reduces dependency on sub-contractors, therefore reducing the risks associated with third-party contractors to a manageable proportion — enabling the company to guarantee quality, service, and on-time delivery.

The company takes pride in delivering work to the highest quality and standards that strictly conform to international standards.

Now IMDAD prepares to obtain DEWA, SEWA, and FIC approval certification, with membership at the Institute of Electrical Engineers (IEE) and the IEEE. The company's engineers have many years of combined experience in the industry with qualifications ranging from Bachelor's to PhD in all subjects under the heading of MEP.

The company begins by working closely with clients to identify their specific needs and goals. Next, it assembles an interdisciplinary team of professionals tailored to each project. This team is directed by a principal and a project manager, who together ensure each assignment is handled by experienced, qualified professionals.

The company manages virtually all aspects of any project with internal resources, providing planning, coordination, and orderly project development. This approach to project management has enabled the company to develop positive client relationships that extend over many years of working together.

## 5. STRATEGY, MISSION & VISION
===================================================================

### Our Strategy
The desire for diversification was not merely focused on multiple technologies, but also on multiple disciplines and territories. The net result of this strategy would minimize the inherent risk of a common myopic focus many companies get settled into. Though change is usually viewed as painful, it is also invigorating as the components of the vision begin to fall into place.

**Plan to execute this strategy was focused on three areas:**
1. Expanding our Core Competencies
2. Identifying and producing unique technologies
3. Aligning with key partners that would enhance our capabilities to produce and distribute these new ideas

Core strengths in the electro-mechanical sector have allowed the company to nurture its business and seek other areas for profitable and sustainable growth. Over a 45-year period, the company (IMDAD Group / Al Jazira for Smart Imdad) has evolved to become a multi-disciplined EPIC service provider. The plan to expand services across a range of engineering and construction disciplines has served the company well.

### Our Mission
To safely deliver world-class Quality MEP projects and Infrastructure projects on time and promoting environmental protection and sustainable communities development.

**(Extended Mission — current Al Jazira version):**
At Al Jazira for Smart Imdad, our mission is to deliver world-class MEP and infrastructure projects while ensuring complete safety and protecting lives. We are committed to completing projects on time, adhering strictly to the highest standards of engineering and technical quality.

Through these principles, the company aims to be a trusted partner for all its clients, providing real value in every project undertaken, and consistently achieving engineering excellence that reflects the company's vision and core values.

### Our Vision
To become a regional market leader in the MEP Construction Industry, providing engineering excellence to valued clients, through commitment to delivering best practices in corporate governance and transparency.

Utilizing proven expertise and experience in designing and building smelting equipment, the approach is to transform the Company into a world-class business through entrepreneurial management and sustained investment.

Operating efficiency — underpinned by tight financial control, judicious use of capital, and strong balance sheets — remains paramount to the Company's long-term security.

At the same time, the company is a very customer-focused organization, constantly adapting itself and what it produces to the changing needs of its customers.
